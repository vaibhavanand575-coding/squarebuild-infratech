Our Story

Squarebuild Infratech Pvt. Ltd.

Squarebuild Infratech was founded with a simple premise: buyers deserve properties that hold their value because they were built and located well — not just marketed well. What started as a single plotted development has grown into a portfolio spanning residential plots, villas, farmhouses and commercial spaces.

Every project we take on is anchored to a growth corridor — an upcoming highway, an expressway, or in the case of our flagship township, the upcoming Jewar International Airport — so that early buyers benefit from genuine infrastructure-led appreciation rather than speculative pricing.
